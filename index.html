v0.9.5 • CAMPAIGN PHASES FOUNDATION

THE CAMPAIGN NOW HAS A ROADMAP.

🗺️
Phases & Sections

Campaign is now divided into a five-fight Prologue, five-fight Phase 1 and five-fight Phase 2, each split into named Sections.

🥊
Prologue — Neo City Arena

Nightfang remains Fight 1. Voltage, Razor, Titan and Arena Champion are reserved as original Fight Arena characters. No MCU villains enter the Prologue.

🏙️
Phase 1 — Street Criminals

Crossbones, Bullseye, Punisher, Taskmaster and Kingpin are the permanent lineup. Punisher is planned to unlock as a playable fighter when his encounter is built and defeated.

🐙
Phase 2 — Sinister Threat

Rhino, Electro, Mysterio, Green Goblin and Doctor Octopus form the single Spider-Man villain Phase. Doctor Octopus is reserved as the Phase Boss.

📦
Standby Vault

Existing villains without a chosen Phase are hidden from the Campaign but fully preserved in source so they can return instantly when their Phase is decided. Nothing was deleted.

🛠️
Foundation-only release

No new villain models, abilities or combat kits are introduced in v0.9.5. The next v0.9.5.x update can start filling the roadmap one villain at a time.

👆
v0.9.4.1 stability retained

Strict Safari combat input, Arena cleanup, Mysterio clone protection and the reorganized Collection remain intact underneath the new Campaign layer.

🐾
v0.9.4 heroes retained

Black Panther, Kinetic Panther and Wolverine remain unchanged with their full abilities, suits and Training systems.
