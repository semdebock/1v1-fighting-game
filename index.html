UPDATE LOG

v0.9.5.1 • NEO CITY ARENA EXPANSION

THE PROLOGUE IS NOW A REAL FIGHTING JOURNEY.

⚡
Voltage — Fight 2

A lean neon-tech runner with capacitor gauntlets, Spark Shot, Static Dash and Shock Pulse. His fight introduces ranged pressure without overwhelming beginners.

🗡️
Razor — Fight 3

A crimson twin-blade rushdown duelist with Twin Slash, Razor Leap and Spin Cut. He teaches blocking, timing and how to answer fast pressure.

🦾
Titan — Fight 4

A huge industrial bruiser with reinforced armor, Heavy Smash, Armored Charge and Ground Slam. He teaches spacing, patience and punishing slow attacks.

🏆
Arena Champion — Prologue Boss

A premium gold-and-black all-rounder with Champion Strike, Victory Rush, Shockwave Kick and Crown of the Arena. Below 45% HP he activates the faster Champion Mode.

🎓
Beginner Journey

Every Prologue fight now has a short pre-fight lesson: fundamentals, ranged awareness, block timing, spacing and finally a boss exam that combines the mechanics.

🎁
Prologue Rewards

Fight rewards scale from 150 to 500 coins. Arena Champion drops 5 diamonds, and the first full Prologue clear grants an extra 750 coins and 5 diamonds.

🗺️
Phases & Sections retained

Phase 1 Street Criminals and Phase 2 Sinister Threat stay visible as the roadmap. Existing assigned villains remain preserved while the missing fights are built upward.

📦
Standby Vault retained

Ultron and Prowler remain fully preserved off-screen. Nothing was deleted from their combat data.

👆
Combat stability retained

Safari anti-zoom input, multi-touch combat, Arena cleanup, Mysterio clone protection and the reorganized Collection stay intact.

🗺️
v0.9.5 Phases Foundation

Campaign is divided into a five-fight Prologue, five-fight Phase 1 and five-fight Phase 2, each split into named Sections.
