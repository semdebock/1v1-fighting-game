v0.9.4 • WAKANDA & WEAPON X

TWO HEROES. TWO COMPLETELY DIFFERENT FIGHT STYLES.

🐾
Black Panther — A+ Rank

3,200 coins. Vibranium Claws, Panther Pounce, Kinetic Absorb and Kinetic Release create an absorb-and-release playstyle built around timing.

💜
Kinetic Panther — 80 💎 Ability Variant

A charged purple Wakandan suit with its own moveset: Kinetic Claw Wave, Vibranium Rush, Perfect Absorb and the stronger Kinetic Overdrive special.

🐺
Wolverine — S Rank

4,000 coins. Classic yellow-and-blue comic suit, Adamantium Slash, Feral Lunge, Healing Surge, passive Healing Factor and Berserker Rage.

🎨
Suit & animation focus

Both new fighters receive dedicated silhouettes, masks, claws, suit details, movement animations, impact effects and special-attack VFX rather than recolored base models.

🎯
Hero-specific Training Data

Black Panther shows live Kinetic Energy. Wolverine tracks Healing Recovered alongside Last Hit, Combo Damage, DPS and the existing Training statistics.

🏆
Expanded ranked roster

S Rank now contains Iron Man, Wolverine and Spider-Man. A+ contains Black Panther, Moon Knight and Captain America. Existing price hierarchy stays intact.

✨
Fighter introductions

Campaign fights now open with a short Arena intro showing the selected hero’s rank, power class and combat role.

👆
Touch Hotfix retained

Rapid combat taps remain protected from Safari double-tap and gesture zoom on iPhone and iPad.

💎
DIAMONDS code retained

DIAMONDS still grants +100 diamonds once per save. Nanotech Iron Man remains the premium 100-diamond Ability Variant.
