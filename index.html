UPDATE LOG

v0.9.3.2 • TOUCH CONTROLS HOTFIX

SPAM ATTACKS. NOT SAFARI ZOOM.

👆
iPhone & iPad anti-zoom combat

Rapid taps on Punch, Kick, Special, Block and hero abilities now suppress Safari double-tap/gesture zoom while preserving normal menu and Collection scrolling.

💎
New code: DIAMONDS

Redeem DIAMONDS once per save to instantly receive +100 diamonds. Your existing save, fighters, skins, ranks and device settings stay unchanged.

🏆
Ranked Roster

Fighters are now grouped clearly from S Rank down to B Rank. Higher ranks have higher coin prices, while every fighter keeps a distinct gameplay role.

⚖️
Power Classes & Balance Pass

Street, Heavy, Enhanced, Mystic, Superhuman and Elite Tech classes now separate lore power from Arena usefulness. Iron Man and Spider-Man rise in raw power while Daredevil leans harder into speed and counter skill.

🔴
Nanotech Iron Man — 100 💎

Nanotech is now the first Ability Variant skin. It equips Nano Cannon, Shoulder Barrage, Nano Wings and the multi-hit Nanotech Arsenal special, with a much more distinct weaponized armor design.

🎯
Training Combat Data

Training now tracks Last Hit, Combo Damage and DPS alongside Total Damage, Hits and Dummy KOs for easier future balancing.

📱
iPhone Portrait Mode

A dedicated vertical layout with a compact arena, portrait-friendly HUD, left-thumb joystick and touch combat controls designed to fit without horizontal scrolling.

📲
iPad / Tablet Mode

The current touch-first layout is refined for larger screens with comfortable controls, larger arena space and three-column Collection browsing.

🖥️
Desktop Mode + Keyboard

A/D move, W or Space jump, J punch, K kick, L special, Shift block, 1–3 hero abilities and Escape pause. Mouse controls remain available.

🤖
Auto Detect + Saved Choice

Choose Auto, iPhone, iPad/Tablet or Desktop on first launch. The choice is saved and can always be changed from Settings.

🛠️
Button & navigation fixes

Collection, Campaign Fight and other main navigation buttons now use the validated static core build directly, removing the deployment mismatch that caused taps to fail.

💎
Cosmetics System

Diamonds can now be spent on permanent skins. Preview, buy, own and equip cosmetics from the new Collection screen. Skins never change combat stats.

🕷️
Five launch cosmetics

Symbiote Spider-Man, Nanotech Iron Man, Hydra Captain America, Golden Champion El Primo and Neo City Fighter Rookie are now available.

😈
Daredevil joins the roster

A premium technical martial artist with Billy Club Throw, Grapple Rush, Radar Counter and the multi-hit Devil's Combo special.

🌙
Moon Knight joins the roster

A premium hybrid vigilante with Crescent Dart, Cape Dive, Moon Guard and the heavy Vengeance of Khonshu special.

🗂️
Character Gallery becomes Collection

Fighters and Skins now live together with rarity presentation, animated Legendary cards and large cosmetic previews.

💾
Safe save migration

Existing fighters, campaign progress, coins, diamonds, settings and both redeem codes are retained. Cosmetic ownership and equipped skins are now saved too.
